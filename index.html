The problem

The sale is lost in the inbox, not in the market.

A social seller in Sri Lanka runs their whole business inside a chat thread. The demand is already there. What breaks is everything that happens after the message arrives.

Enquiries go cold

Messages land at 11pm, during a delivery run, or fifty at once after a promotion post. The ones that wait an hour for "price eka kiyada?" simply buy elsewhere.

Orders are retyped by hand

Name, phone, address, size, quantity, copied out of a chat into a notebook or a spreadsheet every evening. That is where wrong sizes and wrong addresses are born.

Nothing is left behind

No customer history, no record of what sold, no city data for the courier. Growth means hiring another person to answer the same questions again.

What Ordo is

An assistant that speaks like a shop, and files like a system.

Ordo connects to the seller's existing WhatsApp Business, Messenger and Instagram accounts through Meta's official API, the same number, the same page, no new app for the customer to learn. It reads the message, answers it in the customer's own language, and quietly builds the order in the background.

The product is the order, not the chat. A reply is easy to copy. The extracted order, the alias table that knows "රෙද්ද" and "redda" and "reddha" mean the same item in this seller's catalogue, the customer history and the courier city mapping, those are not.

  • Sinhala, Singlish and English, handled the way customers actually type, mixed script and all.
  • Voice notes and photos, a spoken order or a picture of the item becomes text the system can act on.
  • Your catalogue, imported once, a CSV becomes searchable products with automatic name variants.
  • Courier-ready export, confirmed orders leave as a clean file, not a screenshot of a chat.
  • A manual inbox underneath, if anything fails, the seller still sees every message and replies by hand.

How it is built

Four rules we do not bend.

Automation touching someone's income has to be conservative in specific, boring ways. These are the ones that decide what ships.

The model decides language. Code decides money.

The AI is never shown a price, so it cannot leak one. Every rupee figure in a reply is placed by the system from the seller's own catalogue. Any figure that was not is blocked before sending.

The AI never confirms an order.

It goes as far as a complete draft and stops. A human confirms, always. Bank slips are read to save typing, never accepted as proof of payment.

Official Meta API only.

No unofficial WhatsApp libraries, ever. A banned number is a destroyed business, and that risk is not ours to take with someone else's livelihood.

Degrade to a usable inbox, never to nothing.

Provider outage, expired token, exhausted credit, the seller still sees their messages and can still reply. Every seller's data is isolated at the database level, not by careful coding.
